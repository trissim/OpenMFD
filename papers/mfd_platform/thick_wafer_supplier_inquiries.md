# Thick 150 mm Silicon Wafer Supplier Inquiries

Last updated: 2026-09-01

## Target specification

- Diameter: 150 mm (6 inch)
- Thickness: ideally 1,200-1,500 um; up to 2,000 um is acceptable
- Surface: one polished, bare silicon face suitable for SU-8 spin coating and photolithography
- Edge: standard 57.5 mm primary flat preferred
- Condition: no major chips or cracks
- Acceptable grades: test, mechanical, reclaim, surplus, or cosmetically rejected
- Unimportant properties: orientation, dopant, resistivity, and backside finish
- Quantities to quote: 5, 10, and 25 wafers
- Target price: below US$40 per wafer
- Destination: Montreal, Canada
