# Supplementary Table S2. Design parameters and adaptation

Shared dimensions connect the photomasks, alignment pins, insert skirts and
frame. The nominal design dimensions describe the neuronal device before
parylene coating; the pin-height range is a measurement from one printed array.
When adapting a layout, assess printing accuracy, alignment, adhesive sealing,
demolding, final dimensions and assay performance together.

| Feature | Values and relationships used here |
|---|---|
| Device and well spacing | 6 × 8 two-well units (48 devices, 96 wells), spaced 18 × 9 mm apart. Well centers are at ±4.5 mm from each unit center, giving a 9 mm well grid. |
| Device, glass, and wafer | 108 × 72 mm device array; 110 × 74 mm glass with a designed 1 mm margin on each side; 150 mm wafer with a 57.5 mm flat. Cutting and frame outlines derive from the same dimensions. |
| Wells, chambers, and channels | 2.5 mm well radius; 5.0 mm chamber width; 62 channels, each 10 µm wide with 30 µm gaps. The 2.45 mm-wide channel array leaves a 1.275 mm chamber margin on each side. |
| Photolithographic layers | 5 µm patterned SU-8 microchannels over a flood-exposed base; 200 µm SUEX wells and alignment holes. Matching solid and hollow marks align the patterned layers. |
| Pin–hole clearance | 1.85 × 1.85 mm printed pins in 2.0 × 2.0 mm SUEX holes: 150 µm total clearance, or 75 µm per side. |
| Array seating | A 116 µm pin-height range was measured in one array. Compressible EPDM backing accommodates the height differences during collective bonding. |
| Insert taper | 3.8 mm insert height; 16° outer taper; 0.300 mm extra taper allowance. Each side narrows by 1.39 mm from bottom to top to support demolding while retaining wall thickness. |
| Working volume | Up to 50 µL per well in the present plate. Well width, depth, taper, and interwell wall thickness fit within the 9 mm grid and selected glass footprint. Larger wells would require larger, more expensive custom-cut glass. |
| Skirt and adhesive contact | Two-step skirt: 0.75 × 0.66 mm plus 0.80 × 0.04 mm. The recessed contact region is approximately 0.75–0.80 mm wide and keeps adhesive away from the channels. |
| PDMS shrinkage | Matching masks and inserts receive the same 1.0226 horizontal scale factor (2.26% enlargement) for the 100°C cure. |
| Parylene coating | 1 µm parylene C. A uniform coating would add approximately 2 µm to ridge width while leaving ridge height relative to the coated base approximately unchanged. Final cast dimensions also depend on PDMS shrinkage; the generator specifies dimensions before coating. |

## S2B. Figure 5 layouts

The neuronal device was fabricated and used for culture. The three additional
layouts are generated fabrication-file examples. All use a 2.5 mm well radius;
new materials or layouts require their own fabrication and assay assessment.

| Layout | Device spacing, array, and channels |
|---|---|
| Demonstrated neuronal device | 18 × 9 mm; 6 × 8 two-well units; 62 channels, each 10 µm wide with 30 µm gaps. |
| Myelination-inspired | 18 × 18 mm; 6 × 4 units; 125 channels at 10 µm plus an oligodendrocyte branch. |
| Axon guidance | 18 × 18 mm; 6 × 4 units; 83 channels at 10 µm per arm in an orthogonal-gradient layout. |
| Three compartments | 27 × 9 mm; 4 × 8 units; two successive arrays of 125 channels at 10 µm each. |
