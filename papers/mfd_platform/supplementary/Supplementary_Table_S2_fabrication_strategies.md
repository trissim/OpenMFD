# Supplementary Table S2. Multiscale microfluidic fabrication strategies

Representative strategies for combining micrometer-scale features with millimeter-scale wells or other tall structures are summarized below. The comparison emphasizes the operations and equipment each strategy adds to conventional PDMS soft lithography.

| Approach | Micrometer-scale features | Millimeter-scale wells or tall structures | Plate-format and automation compatibility | Added workflow and equipment | Representative references |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conventional PDMS soft lithography with manual punching | SU-8 photolithography for micrometer-scale channels | Openings made with a biopsy punch after casting | Each opening is made separately after casting | Biopsy punching and manual debris removal during every device-production cycle | Baseline approach; see Taylor et al., 2005 |
| CO2 laser micromachining of PDMS | Maskless laser-ablated trenches whose profile depends on power, pulse density, and traverse speed | Laser-ablated PDMS channels rather than cast well features | Computer-controlled but serial processing of PDMS sheets | CO2 laser processing during each device-production cycle | Holle et al., 2007 |
| Hybrid micro- and millimeter-scale mold via milling and hot embossing | Hot embossing for microfeatures | PMMA-milled large structures | Batch device production from a reusable PDMS mold | Milling, hot embossing, and a workflow involving several molds | Park et al., 2010 |
| Thick SU-8 molding and photolithography | Photolithography on a molded SU-8 surface | Millimeter structures molded from a 3D-printed pattern through an intermediate PDMS mold | Continuous micro- and millimeter-scale structures | Printed-pattern preparation, intermediate molding, and multi-step SU-8 handling | Tamura and Suzuki, 2019 |
| Direct 3D printing of PDMS onto a photolithographic mold | Photolithographic mold for microchannels | Printed PDMS walls or compartments | Automated deposition of selected large structures | PDMS-printing hardware, material formulation, and print control during production | Kajtez et al., 2020 |
| Resin-printed molds and parts retained in the finished device | Printed molds form the PDMS microstructures | A separate printed cap defines and closes the culture chambers | Eight parallel chambers aligned to a 96-well plate grid for robotic and microscope compatibility | Printer and resin selection, post-treatment, mixed-material assembly, and perfusion hardware | Meynard et al., 2025 |
| Aligned resin printing onto patterned chips | SU-8 lithography on diced chips | Resin-printed micro- and millimeter-scale 3D features | Aligned structures on individual chips | Chip dicing, resin-printing equipment, and chip-level alignment fixtures | Pan et al., 2022 |
| 3D-printed inserts bonded to SU-8 | SU-8 photolithography for micrometer-scale features | Tall 3D-printed metal inserts bonded with epoxy | Open cell compartments at individual-device scale | Metal 3D printing and individual alignment and bonding of three inserts on each mold | Ristola et al., 2019 |
| Hard-plastic microtiter microfluidic plates | Hot-embossed microchannels in COC | CNC-milled wells or compartments with fusion bonding | ANSI/SLAS-compatible automated plate format | CNC milling, hot embossing, layer alignment, and fusion bonding | Moll et al., 2024 |
| Commercial microfluidic titer plates | Proprietary phaseguide-based microfluidics | Proprietary | 384-well plate footprint containing 40 tissue-culture chips | Commercial procurement and vendor-defined geometries | Spijkers et al., 2021 |
| This work: wafer-bonded resin well inserts with SU-8/SUEX features | SU-8/SUEX photolithography for micrometer-scale features | An aligned resin insert array is bonded once to the reusable mold; all wells form during casting | Standard plate dimensions and 9 mm well grid; demonstrated with high-content imaging and autofocus | Resin-array printing, one-time alignment and bonding, and parylene coating of the reusable mold; repeated device production uses PDMS casting and glass bonding | This work |
