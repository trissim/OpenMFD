# Supplementary Table S5. Hybrid-mold use and PDMS-cast outcomes

This table summarizes mold use with the finalized protocol. Process-development
observations are reported in the Results and Supplementary Notes S1-S2.

| Stage or outcome | Observed count | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Molds assembled after protocol stabilization | 5 | Five wafer-scale molds completed insert bonding and final assembly. |
| Molds entering routine use | 3 | These molds produced usable PDMS casts with open microchannels and retained well features. |
| Wafer assemblies lost during casting or handling | 2 | One wafer broke during demolding. Leaked PDMS cured beneath a second wafer in the casting rack. |
| Usable PDMS casts from the three routinely used molds | More than 50 | Casts demolded intact with open wells, preserved microchannels, bonded inserts, attached SU-8/SUEX features, and full well fidelity. |
| Mold use duration | More than 8 months | The most recent molds remained active for more than 8 months and retained their visible condition at the time of manuscript preparation. |
