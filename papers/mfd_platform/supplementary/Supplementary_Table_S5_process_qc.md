# Supplementary Table S5. Hybrid-mold and PDMS-cast quality-control outcomes

The process-development records support complete denominators for mold outcomes
but not for every PDMS pour. The table therefore reports the observed counts
without estimating an unrecorded cast yield.

| Stage or outcome | Observed count | Denominator and interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Hybrid molds attempted in the reported series | 5 | Five wafer-scale hybrid molds entered insert bonding and downstream handling. |
| Molds completing visible QC and entering routine use | 3 | 3/5 molds had intact SU-8/SUEX features, seated and bonded inserts, no visible epoxy contamination of the microchannel regions, and produced usable PDMS casts. |
| Wafer assemblies lost during downstream mechanical handling | 2 | 2/5 assemblies were mechanically lost during casting or handling: one during PDMS demolding and one while releasing a wafer from the casting rack after leaked PDMS cured beneath the wafer. The retained records attribute these losses to handling rather than observed insert-bond or photolithography failure. |
| Usable PDMS casts from the three passing molds | More than 50 | A usable cast demolded intact, retained open wells and intact microchannel regions, and showed no visible insert-bonding failure, SU-8/SUEX delamination, or loss of well fidelity. |
| Total PDMS casts attempted | Not prospectively recorded | Because every pour and failed cast was not logged during process development, a numerical per-cast yield or confidence interval cannot be calculated from the retained records. |
| Mold use duration | More than 8 months | The most recent passing molds remained in active use for more than 8 months without observable degradation at the time of manuscript preparation. |

Visible QC was performed before biological use. These observations establish the
demonstrated process history but are not a formal lifetime or manufacturing-yield
study.
