# Supplementary Note S1. LP360 long-pass filter improves SUEX K200 top-surface flatness

During development of the 200 µm SUEX K200 layer, unfiltered exposure produced ridge-like artifacts around patterned features. These ridges interrupted the seal between the printed inserts and the mold, allowing epoxy to reach nearby SU-8 features and weakening the insert bond.

Exposure through an LP360 long-pass filter substantially reduced the ridges and produced a flatter bonding surface.
