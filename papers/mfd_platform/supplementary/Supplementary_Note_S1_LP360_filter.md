# Supplementary Note S1. Uneven SUEX surfaces

LP360-filtered exposure of the 200 µm SUEX K200 layer provided a flatter surface for the insert–wafer epoxy seal. Raised edges were visible after unfiltered exposure (Figure S1, left) and substantially reduced after LP360-filtered exposure (Figure S1, right).

![Figure S1. SUEX K200 features after unfiltered exposure (left) and LP360-filtered exposure (right). Ridge-like edge artifacts are visible after unfiltered exposure and substantially reduced with the filter.](../figures/rendered/supp_fig_s1_no_lp360.pdf)
