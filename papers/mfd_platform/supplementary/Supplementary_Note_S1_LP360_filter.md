# Supplementary Note S1. LP360 long-pass filter improves SUEX K200 top-surface flatness

During development of the SUEX K200 (200 µm) layer, we observed that exposure without an LP360 long-pass filter can produce ridge-like edge artifacts around patterned features, resulting in a non-flat top surface.

This surface non-flatness directly compromises the insert-to-wafer adhesive bonding step: when resin inserts are bonded to the wafer mold using low-viscosity epoxy, the ridges prevent a uniform seal at the insert-wafer interface. Poor sealing can lead to epoxy leaking onto the SU-8 microfeatures during bonding, weak mechanical attachment of inserts, and potential insert delamination during PDMS casting or demolding.

Using an LP360 filter (as specified in the main Methods) substantially reduced these artifacts and produced a flatter top surface, enabling reliable epoxy bonding of the resin inserts.
