# Supplementary Note S1. LP360 long-pass filter improves SUEX K200 top-surface flatness

LP360-filtered exposure of the 200 µm SUEX K200 layer provided a flatter surface for the insert–wafer epoxy seal. Compared with unfiltered exposure, it substantially reduced the raised edges around patterned features (Figure S1).

![Supplementary Figure S1. SUEX K200 features after unfiltered exposure (left) and LP360-filtered exposure (right). Ridge-like edge artifacts are visible after unfiltered exposure and substantially reduced with the filter. The images provide a qualitative process-development comparison.](../figures/rendered/supp_fig_s1_no_lp360.pdf)
