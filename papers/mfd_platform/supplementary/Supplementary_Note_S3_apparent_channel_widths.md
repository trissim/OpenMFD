# Supplementary Note S3 — Apparent microchannel widths in cast devices

Brightfield images supplied nine apparent width observations: three channel positions in one device from each of the three plates. Figure S3 shows device R02C02 from Plate 1 (left), Plate 2 (centre) and Plate 3 (right). Distances were read between the centres of opposing dark wall bands using the native image calibration of 1.18675253 µm per pixel. The observed spans were 9–11 pixels, equivalent to 10.7–13.1 µm (Data S4).

The measured distances depend on the chosen centres of the dark wall bands. Moving each endpoint by one native pixel can change a span by up to two pixels, or 2.37 µm at the recorded calibration. This calculation describes sensitivity to endpoint placement. The offset between a dark band and the physical channel wall was not quantified, so the calculation does not bound error in the true physical width. Channel depth and the full cross-sectional profile were not measured.
