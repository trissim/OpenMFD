# Supplementary Note S3 — Apparent microchannel widths in cast devices

Brightfield images supplied nine apparent width observations: three channel positions in one device from each of the three plates. Figure S3 shows device R02C02 from Plate 1 (left), Plate 2 (centre) and Plate 3 (right). Distances were read between the centres of opposing dark wall bands using the native image calibration of 1.18675253 µm per pixel. The observed distances ranged from 10.7 to 13.1 µm. Supplementary Data S4 provides the full-precision values, pixel coordinates, calibration and recorded placement notes. These are planar optical observations; optical boundary bias was not quantified, and channel depth and the full cross-sectional profile were not measured.
