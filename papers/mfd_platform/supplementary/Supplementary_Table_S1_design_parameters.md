# Supplementary Table S1. Device and mold dimensions

Dimensions refer to the neuronal layout before parylene coating. The pin-height range was measured in one printed array; the other values specify the design dimensions.

| Feature | Dimensions |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Plate layout | 6 × 8 two-well units: 48 devices and 96 wells. Unit spacing: 18 × 9 mm; well centres: ±4.5 mm from each unit centre, on a 9 mm grid. |
| Device, glass and wafer | 108 × 72 mm device; 110 × 74 mm glass, with a designed 1 mm margin per side; 150 mm wafer with a 57.5 mm flat. |
| Wells and channels | 2.5 mm well radius; 5.0 mm chamber width; 62 channels, 10 µm wide with 30 µm gaps. Channel-array width: 2.45 mm; chamber margin: 1.275 mm per side. |
| Photolithographic layers | 5 µm patterned SU-8 over a flood-exposed base; 200 µm SUEX wells and alignment holes. Solid and hollow marks align the layers. |
| Alignment pins and holes | 1.85 × 1.85 mm pins; 2.0 × 2.0 mm holes. Designed space between pin and hole: 150 µm total, or 75 µm per side. |
| Pin-height variation | 116 µm range across 96 pins. Compressible EPDM backing accommodates this variation during bonding. |
| Insert height and taper | 3.8 mm height; 16° outer taper; 0.300 mm taper allowance. Each side narrows by 1.39 mm from bottom to top. |
| Well capacity | Up to 50 µL per well within the 9 mm grid and glass footprint. |
| Skirt and adhesive contact | Two-step skirt: 0.75 × 0.66 mm and 0.80 × 0.04 mm. Recessed adhesive-contact width: approximately 0.75–0.80 mm. |
| Shrinkage compensation | Matching masks and inserts: 1.0226 horizontal scale factor, or 2.26% enlargement, for curing at 100°C. |
| Parylene C | 1 µm coating. A uniform film adds approximately 2 µm to ridge width; ridge height relative to the coated base remains approximately unchanged. |

## Alternative layouts

All layouts have a 2.5 mm well radius. Only the neuronal layout was fabricated and used for culture; the other layouts correspond to the fabrication files shown in Figure 5.

- **Neuronal:** 18 × 9 mm unit spacing; 6 × 8 units; 62 channels, 10 µm wide with 30 µm gaps.
- **Myelination:** 18 × 18 mm spacing; 6 × 4 units; 125 channels at 10 µm, plus an oligodendrocyte branch.
- **Axon guidance:** 18 × 18 mm spacing; 6 × 4 units; 83 channels at 10 µm per arm, arranged for gradients at right angles to each other.
- **Three compartments:** 27 × 9 mm spacing; 4 × 8 units; two successive arrays of 125 channels at 10 µm.
