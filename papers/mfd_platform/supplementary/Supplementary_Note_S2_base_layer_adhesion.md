# Supplementary Note S2. SU-8 base-layer adhesion and pre-SUEX hard bake

A 5 µm flood-exposed, undeveloped SU-8 2005 base layer supports the patterned microchannels through subsequent processing. Both micrographs in Figure S2 (left and right) show the delamination observed without this layer. The approximately 10 µm two-layer stack is then baked at 150°C for 10 min before SUEX lamination, using direct oven transfer without controlled heating or cooling. In process-development observations, part of an eight-wafer batch without this bake delaminated after SUEX processing; both wafers in the subsequent batch with the bake remained intact.
