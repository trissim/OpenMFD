# Supplementary Note S2. SU-8 base-layer adhesion and pre-SUEX hard bake

The 5 µm SU-8 2005 base layer is exposed across the whole wafer without a mask and left undeveloped to support the patterned channel-forming features. Figure S2 shows two examples of delamination when these features were patterned without the base layer.

Before SUEX lamination, the approximately 10 µm two-layer stack is baked at 150°C for 10 min, with direct oven transfer and no controlled heating or cooling ramp. In the process-development observations, part of an eight-wafer batch without this bake delaminated after SUEX processing, while both wafers in a two-wafer batch with the bake remained intact.
