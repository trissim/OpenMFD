# Supplementary data and instrument files

- **Data S1 — Insert-pin heights.** The [96 measurements](pin_z_variability_raw_mm.csv) are arranged by array row (A–H) and column (1–12), in millimetres. [Deviations from the array mean](pin_z_variability_delta_um.csv) are in micrometres.
- **Data S2 — Resin-insert printing.** The [printer profile](resin_insert_printing_settings.csv) contains the resin, exposure, motion, timing and slicer settings. Protocol S2 describes cleaning and post-curing.
- **Data S3 — Frame printing.** The [HIPS profile](HIPS_frame_printing_settings.csv) and [G-code](UPGRADE_96_well_plate_reservoirs_print_hips_2_chamfered_v2_HIPS_3h36m.gcode) contain the frame-printing settings.
- **Data S4 — Apparent channel widths.** The [nine measurements](validation/channel_widths.csv) include pixel coordinates, calibration and full-precision distances. Brightfield crops from [Plate 1](validation/P1_width_crop.npy), [Plate 2](validation/P2_width_crop.npy) and [Plate 3](validation/P3_width_crop.npy) are supplied as NumPy arrays. The [crop coordinates](validation/channel_width_crop_bounds.csv) locate them in the source images; `x0,y0` are inclusive and `x1,y1` exclusive bounds. Figure S3 shows the measurement positions.
- **Data S5 — Cell counts.** [Per-device CTB counts](validation/ctb_device_counts.csv) include all 72 interior devices and the five saved flags. The [multi-endpoint table](../supplementary_bio/aggregated_data.csv) contains the same pre-axotomy counts in `pre_cell_count`, with three `pre_outlier` flags, alongside the other endpoints.
- **Data S6 — Fabrication methods.** The [comparison](fabrication_strategies.csv) lists the feature-forming methods, well formation, production format and additional equipment for eleven fabrication approaches. References are given with each approach.

The [Opera Phenix acquisition file](Opera_Phenix_acquisition_index.xml) gives the microscope settings and image positions for Plate 1. Protocol S1 describes acquisition and alignment to a common counting area.
