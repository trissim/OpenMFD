# Supplementary data and instrument files

The following files accompany the article separately from the typeset Supplementary Information. They preserve the full measurements and printer profiles for reuse.

- **Supplementary Data S1 — Resin-insert pin heights.** [pin_z_variability_raw_mm.csv](pin_z_variability_raw_mm.csv) contains the 96 micrometer measurements in millimeters, arranged by array row (A–H) and column (1–12). [pin_z_variability_delta_um.csv](pin_z_variability_delta_um.csv) gives each pin's deviation from the mean in micrometers. Across this array, the mean height was 5.551 mm, the sample standard deviation was 26.7 µm, and the range was 116 µm (minimum 5.483 mm; maximum 5.599 mm).
- **Supplementary Data S2 — Resin-insert printer profile.** [resin_insert_printing_settings.csv](resin_insert_printing_settings.csv) contains the printer, resin, exposure, motion, timing, and slicer settings. Cleaning and UV post-curing are described in Protocol S2.
- **Supplementary Data S3 — HIPS frame printer profile.** [HIPS_frame_printing_settings.csv](HIPS_frame_printing_settings.csv) contains the printer, material, temperature, layer, extrusion, and motion settings. The accompanying [Creality Print 6.0.4 G-code file](UPGRADE_96_well_plate_reservoirs_print_hips_2_chamfered_v2_HIPS_3h36m.gcode) contains the complete frame-printing configuration.

- **Supplementary Data S4 — Apparent microchannel widths.** [channel_widths.csv](validation/channel_widths.csv) supplies all nine measurements from three devices, including native pixel coordinates, calibration, full-precision distances and recorded placement notes. Supplementary Figure S3 shows the placements in brightfield images. The retained [Plate 1](validation/P1_width_crop.npy), [Plate 2](validation/P2_width_crop.npy) and [Plate 3](validation/P3_width_crop.npy) image crops are supplied as NumPy arrays. Their [native crop bounds](validation/channel_width_crop_bounds.csv) identify each crop's position in the source image: `x0` and `y0` are its starting pixel coordinates, and `x1` and `y1` are its exclusive upper bounds. The measurements describe apparent opposing wall-band-centre distances, not channel depth or a complete cross-sectional profile; optical boundary bias was not quantified.
- **Supplementary Data S5 — Retained per-device counts and saved flags.** [ctb_device_counts.csv](validation/ctb_device_counts.csv) contains all 72 retained pre-axotomy CTB counts, device identities and the five saved exclusion flags with their recorded reasons. The earlier [aggregated_data.csv](../supplementary_bio/aggregated_data.csv) retains the same 72 pre-axotomy counts in its `pre_cell_count` column and three `pre_outlier` flags; its other endpoint columns are preserved, not substituted for the CTB readout. Applying these two saved flag sets would retain 67 or 69 devices, respectively. The intended total remains 69, but the additional two flags and endpoint-specific exclusion rationale require author confirmation. These files preserve the retained exports; neither is established as the measurement source for the current Figure 4C, whose plate ranking differs from both saved pre-count summaries. No counts or flags were changed to prepare this supplement.

The accompanying [Opera Phenix XML file](Opera_Phenix_acquisition_index.xml) contains the microscope settings and image positions for Plate 1. Protocol S1 describes the acquisition and the common image area used for counting across plates.
