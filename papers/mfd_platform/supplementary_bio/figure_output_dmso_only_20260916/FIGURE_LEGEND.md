**Supplementary Figure S1. Plate-format microfluidic devices and quantification of axon regeneration in three DMSO-control plates.** Primary cortical neurons from one E18 rat embryo were cultured across three technical replicate plates. Axons extended from the soma compartment through microchannels into the axon compartment. CTβ-647 was added to the axon compartment on day 10 in vitro to label neurons whose axons had reached that compartment. Cell bodies were imaged after 24 h, before chemical axotomy on day 11. Axons in the axon compartment were removed with trypsin and Triton X-100; regeneration was assessed 72 h later using calcein-AM imaging.

**(A)** Plate layout, single-device schematic, and three-dimensional PDMS device rendering. The plate contains 48 two-compartment devices with 96 wells on the standard 9 mm well grid, allowing multichannel pipetting and high-content imaging. The highlighted interior region contains the 24 devices assessed on each plate (72 devices in total). Microchannels connect the soma and axon compartments. Schematic scale bar, 5 mm.

**(B)** CTβ-positive cell counts identify the neurons whose axons reached the axon compartment before axotomy. **(C)** Total regenerating-axon skeleton length in the axon compartment at the 72 h endpoint, obtained by tracing calcein-AM-positive axons. **(D)** Hoechst 33342-positive nuclear counts provide a measure of cellular loading. **(E)** Total regenerating-axon skeleton length divided by the corresponding CTβ-positive cell count, expressing regeneration relative to the number of initially connected neurons. Image-pixel lengths were converted to micrometers using the Opera Phenix acquisition calibration (1.18675 µm/pixel); units are µm in C and µm/cell in E. Bars show mean ± SD, and points represent individual devices. Blue, orange, and green identify Plates 1, 2, and 3, respectively. Five devices with cell-counting artifacts were excluded from the CTβ-dependent summaries in B and E (two on Plate 1 and three on Plate 2); their values are retained as red crosses. Included sample sizes are 22, 21, and 24 devices in B and E, and 24 per plate in C and D.

**(F)** Left: representative CTβ images of axon-compartment microchannel exits acquired immediately after axotomy in separate, pre-treatment acquisitions. These images illustrate cut verification: intact axons should no longer extend continuously from the channel exits into the axon compartment. Right: mosaics of all 24 assessed devices from each DMSO-control plate, arranged by plate position. Each device pairs the CTβ-positive cell-body image with its regenerating-axon image; yellow overlays show cell segmentation and axon tracing. Red outlines identify the devices excluded from B and E.

**(G)** Enlarged representative devices from each plate, shown without overlays (Untraced) and with yellow cell-segmentation and axon-tracing overlays (Traced). Each pair contains the CTβ-positive cell-body image on the left and the 72 h regenerating-axon image on the right. Background correction and contrast adjustment of the CTβ images were used for display only and did not change the measurements. Scale bars, 500 µm. Images were stitched and analyzed with OpenHCS, version 0.5.15. CTβ, cholera toxin subunit β; DMSO, dimethyl sulfoxide; PDMS, polydimethylsiloxane.
