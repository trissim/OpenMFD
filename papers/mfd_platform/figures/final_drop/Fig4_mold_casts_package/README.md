This panel summarizes the routine post-mold replication and assembly workflow:
cast PDMS within an aluminum-foil reservoir, degas and cure molds in the
six-shelf wafer rack, demold feature-side up, tape the circular cast to paper
along the molded cutting walls, trim it with a paper guillotine, plasma bond to
glass and autoclave the bonded PDMS-glass device, ethanol-decontaminate the HIPS
frame, invert the frame to fill its upward-facing groove, place the inverted
bonded device with the glass facing upward, cure the adhesive for 3 days, turn
the assembly upright, and perform the final plasma clean before
coating/culture. It intentionally excludes
full mold fabrication, cell culture, and axotomy steps. One-time hybrid mold
fabrication is covered in Supplementary Protocol S2, and culture/axotomy steps
are covered in Supplementary Protocol S1.
