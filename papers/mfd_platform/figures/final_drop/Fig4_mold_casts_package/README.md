This panel summarizes the routine post-mold replication and assembly workflow:
cast PDMS, demold/trim/clean, plasma bond to glass, autoclave the bonded
PDMS-glass device, ethanol-sterilize the HIPS frame, glue/cure the assembly, and
perform the final plasma clean before coating/culture. It intentionally excludes
full mold fabrication, cell culture, and axotomy steps. One-time hybrid mold
fabrication is covered in Supplementary Protocol S2, and culture/axotomy steps
are covered in Supplementary Protocol S1.
