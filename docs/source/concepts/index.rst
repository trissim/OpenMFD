Overview
--------

OpenMFD is built around several core concepts:

* **Wells**: Circular or square reservoirs for cell culture
* **Channels**: Microfluidic channels connecting compartments
* **Chambers**: Larger compartments for cell growth
* **Devices**: Complete assemblies of wells, channels, and chambers
* **Export**: Generation of SCAD, DXF, and STL files
